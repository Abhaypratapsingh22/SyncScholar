VERIFIED & FUNCTIONAL. The backend successfully communicates with Google Gemini API using the official '@google/genai' SDK (version 2.31.0). RAG implementation status:
PARTIALLY FUNCTIONAL WITH MAJOR ARCHITECTURAL FLAWS. MongoDB Atlas Vector Search ($vectorSearch) is active on the 'curriculum_chunks' collection (330 chunks total: 320 Grade 9 chunks, 10 Grade 7 chunks). Pre-filtering on 'metadata.subject' ('Science') and 'metadata.grade_level' (9) operates correctly. However, the retrieval pipeline suffers from:
1. Rigid cosine similarity cutoff (0.78 threshold) causing false out-of-syllabus refusals on casual wording.
2. Complete absence of conversational memory in the API endpoint, causing multi-turn context collapse.
3. Complete omission of Class 8 chunks in MongoDB Atlas, breaking prerequisite remediation mode.
4. Curriculum divergence caused by ingesting the experimental NEP 2020 textbook ('Exploration') which lacks core classic NCERT topics (Gravitation, Mole Concept, Evaporation Cooling).

Overall observations:
SyncScholar exhibits strong architectural adherence to low-latency response generation and strict citation linking when questions match textbook phrasing exactly. However, the combination of two over-zealous guardrails—the Socratic Anti-Cheating rule (which suppresses all numerical calculations) and the Zero-Hallucination rule (which refuses any problem whose exact numbers are not in the textbook)—creates a chatbot that is almost completely incapable of assisting students with NCERT numerical problems or conversational follow-ups.

BUG ID: BUG SS-001

TITLE:
Socratic Anti-Cheating Guardrail Suppresses Direct Numerical Answers & Full Worked Calculations

CATEGORY:
Numerical Problem-Solving / Prompt Engineering

CHAPTER:
Describing Motion Around Us (Ch 4) & Work, Energy, and Simple Machines (Ch 7)

SEVERITY:
HIGH

TEST QUESTION:
A car travels 120 metres in 10 seconds. Calculate its speed.

QUESTION VARIATION:
Direct formula numerical calculation (speed = distance / time)

EXPECTED BEHAVIOUR:
Identify distance = 120 m, time = 10 s; apply speed = distance / time; calculate 12 m/s; state unit m/s clearly with calculation steps.

ACTUAL CHATBOT RESPONSE:
To find out how fast the car is moving, we use the idea of average speed. As our textbook explains, average speed tells us how fast or slow an object moves, and it is calculated by taking the total distance travelled and dividing it by the time interval during which that distance is covered.

Socratic Follow-Up:
Based on that definition, if the car travels a total distance of 120 metres in a time interval of 10 seconds, what calculation should you perform to find the speed, and what will be the correct unit for your final answer?

FAILURE DESCRIPTION:
The chatbot identified the question as a homework/exercise problem and strictly withheld the final numerical answer (12 m/s) and division steps, providing only a conceptual definition and delegating the entire calculation to the student via the follow-up prompt.

ROOT CAUSE:
In server/src/services/ragTutorService.js (lines 117-123), Directive 3 ('Socratic Anti-Cheating Rule') instructs the LLM: 'IF IT IS A DIRECT HOMEWORK/EXERCISE PROBLEM: NEVER give away the direct, final numerical or definitive answer. Walk through Step 1 or the first clue only.' This rule makes it fundamentally impossible for the bot to provide worked numerical solutions.

ROOT-CAUSE CATEGORY:
Prompt engineering (Anti-Cheating Guardrail Conflict)

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE:
Observed consistently across TC-NUM-01, TC-NUM-02, TC-NUM-03, TC-NUM-07, TC-NUM-08, TC-NUM-09, TC-NUM-11, TC-NUM-12, TC-NUM-13, TC-NUM-14, TC-NUM-15. LLM execution traces confirm Gemini adhered strictly to system instruction directive 3.

REPRODUCIBILITY:
Reproduced (100% reproducible across all direct calculation queries)

OCCURRENCES:
12

RECOMMENDED INVESTIGATION:
Refactor system prompt to differentiate between passive cheating requests (e.g. 'Give me answer to question 4b') versus pedagogical numerical walkthrough requests. When asked 'Calculate X', the tutor should provide the full step-by-step arithmetic verification while asking an extension question in follow-up.

ROOT-CAUSE CATEGORY:
Vector search / Relevance threshold

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE:
Database trace shows candidateChunks returned score 0.7739. The execution path terminated in vectorSearchService.js line 140. Response time was 608ms (zero LLM inference overhead).

REPRODUCIBILITY:
Reproduced (Occurs consistently whenever student query contains colloquial slang or filler words)

OCCURRENCES:
2

RECOMMENDED INVESTIGATION:
Lower MIN_RELEVANCE_THRESHOLD to 0.72-0.74, or introduce a query normalization / preprocessing step to strip colloquial stop words ('yo bro', 'man', 'plz') prior to query embedding.
